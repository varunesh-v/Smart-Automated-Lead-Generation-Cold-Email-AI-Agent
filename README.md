# Smart Automated Lead Generation & Cold Email AI Agent

An AI-powered lead generation and cold outreach workflow built with **n8n, Groq API, Google Sheets, and Gmail**.

## What It Does

- Collects leads from Google Sheets
- Scrapes company websites for relevant information
- Uses a Groq-powered AI Agent to generate personalized cold emails
- Sends emails automatically through Gmail
- Updates lead and outreach status in Google Sheets
- Processes **100+ personalized prospects** with **under 30 seconds per prospect**

## Workflow

```text
Google Sheets
      ↓
Website Scraping
      ↓
Data Processing
      ↓
AI Agent + Groq
      ↓
Personalized Email
      ↓
Gmail
      ↓
Status Tracking
```

## Tech Stack

- **n8n** – Workflow automation
- **Groq API** – LLM inference
- **Google Sheets API** – Lead management
- **Gmail API** – Email delivery
- **JavaScript** – Data processing
- **AI Agent + Structured Output Parser** – Email generation

## Key Highlights

- Automated end-to-end lead research and outreach
- Context-aware email personalization using LLMs
- Structured AI outputs for reliable automation
- Lightweight CRM-style lead tracking using Google Sheets
